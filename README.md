A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7) entry for
**"The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert"** — a three-day contest for the best
single satirical sentence about lawmakers who regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert
a file to PDF.

## What it does

`main.py` writes one original satirical sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, 1–320 characters):

> The committee that just voted to regulate artificial intelligence spent the morning debating whether a PDF is a file, a program, or a kind of printer — and the chairman still emails his bills to his secretary to convert.

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
